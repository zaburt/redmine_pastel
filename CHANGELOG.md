## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-10

### Added
- Initial release: a soft, professional theme with pastel blue-grey surfaces and ocean, teal, emerald and maroon accents.
- Support for Redmine 7+ (`themes/` directory).
- Header, login and favicon artwork.
